The AAA's prospective expansion class entered Saturday's political debate appearing sympathetic to **Chance Robinson's campaign for change**. Within minutes, at least one was publicly reconsidering his options.

The turning point was an entirely unauthorized border-security proclamation by co-commissioner **Ryan Mays**, who announced a *“TOTAL AND COMPLETE SHUTDOWN”* of Chance-aligned applicants, pledged extreme vetting, and proposed making Chance pay for the wall with friendship. The stated goal was to protect league democracy. The proclamation also expressly ruled out a vote.

The prospective recruits, **Josh and Brandon**, initially objected to the treatment. Josh called the decision *“Very Democratic of you Mays,”* which the newsroom's constitutional desk has classified as sarcasm.

Then Brandon seemed to identify an unexpected advantage to the current administration's approach.

**“Damn. Maybe Ryan should be the new commish. Sorry Chance.”**

He followed with an unusually candid statement of political philosophy: **“IM DRAWN TO SWIFT DECISION MAKING WITHOUT ASKING ANYONE ANYTHING UNDER THE GUISE OF DEMOCRACY.”**

The irony was not lost on the incumbent. **Casey Hutchins** defended the existing power structure: **“Mays is co-commish for a reason. He holds power as do i. We run this league effectively.”**

Josh, meanwhile, observed that the exchange had become a **“Damning point against the Chance campaign”** and demanded that Chance defend himself. No response from Chance appears in the supplied conversation.

For now, the potential realignment remains just that: **potential**. Nobody has formally endorsed Casey, the proposed recruits have not been admitted, and Casey reiterated that expanding to 12 teams would be put to a vote at the end of the season.

**AAA Insider finding:** The candidates came seeking a more democratic league. After being denied entry without a vote, they began considering the people who denied it. Washington has nothing on fantasy football.
