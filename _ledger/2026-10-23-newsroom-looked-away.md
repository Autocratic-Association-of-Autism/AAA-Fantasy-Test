*By the AAA Insider Newsroom*

AAA Insider stepped away from its publishing desk for several days. The league, displaying a complete disregard for the newspaper's staffing situation, went ahead and played football, conducted trades, and launched another round of commissioner-election theater.

The newsroom has now reconstructed the essential events from ESPN's Week 6 final results, its transaction history through October 23, and GroupMe messages archived through October 20. This is the record, condensed. Those wishing to file further allegations will find that the tip line continues to function without supervision.

## THE UNDEFEATED ARE NO LONGER UNDEFEATED

**Lamborghini Murci** (Ryan Hutchins) defeated **Njigba please** (Chance Robinson), **119.08–77.20**, ending Chance's 5–0 start with a **41.88-point** loss. A commissioner candidate campaigning on strength and change has now experienced both in a single Sunday. Ryan Hutchins, who has never been shy about asking the league to appreciate his achievements, received another opening.

Chance's troubles arrived just as his election campaign was assuring the membership of its superior leadership credentials. The timing was unhelpful. The score was worse.

**Champ** (Glen Hutchins) beat **la porta potty** (Louis Swanson), **108.86–69.72**, a **39.14-point** result that kept Glen atop the standings at 5–1. Louis is 1–5 and in urgent need of either improved football outcomes or a better explanation for the current ones.

## A COMMISSIONER FINALLY WINS A GAME

**ChanceforChange2027**, Casey Hutchins's team at the start of Week 6, defeated Ryan's **Big Ditka Energy**, **132.58–97.38**. After five consecutive losses, the sitting commissioner secured her first win by **35.20 points**. The administrative implications of a margin just over the league's 35-point embarrassment line will presumably be studied at the highest levels.

Ryan entered the game 2–3, faced a strong commissioner performance, and departed at 2–4. This was not the outcome the analytics desk had hoped to explain. It was, however, unusually productive material for the election desk.

Elsewhere, **Warren Hormuz** (Zak Wesolek) posted Week 6's highest total, **146.60**, and still needed most of it to get past **Kenneth Skywalker** (Larry Terrell), **135.74**. The game featured two of the week's three highest scores and one very unfortunate loser. **Coke(r) Caine** (Macon Moore) took care of **Coach** (Frankie Yentz), **117.04–100.90**. Macon moved to 4–2; Frankie fell to 2–4.

## THREE TRADES, ONE VERY ACTIVE DESK

ESPN's completed transaction log confirms that Ryan and Chance finalized their blockbuster on **October 20**: **Bijan Robinson and Brian Robinson Jr.** went to Chance; **Jaxon Smith-Njigba, J.K. Dobbins, and Emanuel Wilson** went to Ryan.

On **October 21**, Ryan moved **Caleb Williams and Hunter Henry** to Ryan Hutchins in return for **Bhayshul Tuten and Rachaad White**. Moments later, **Alvin Kamara and Jameson Williams** went to Macon for **Chuba Hubbard**. Three trades, three negotiating partners, and a roster that will require readers to consult the transaction log rather than rely on muscle memory.

These are confirmed completed deals, not hypothetical offers. Whether they improve everyone's prospects is an entirely separate investigation.

## THE ELECTION WILL NOT LEAVE US ALONE

During an October 17 GroupMe discussion, Chance faced a question about promising larger prizes without higher league dues. His initial answer included the words **“The prize of friendship.”** He later promised **“great prizes.”** The finance department has declined to put a value on friendship while the cash-prize methodology remains unclear.

An anonymous email has also attempted to turn informal discussion of a possible 12-team league into a campaign issue. It alleges that Larry and Frankie oppose expansion; neither man's position has been independently confirmed by the newsroom. Chance supports the idea, while Casey's position remains unclear. No formal expansion proposal has been established.

## THE STATE OF THE LEAGUE

Through Week 6, **Champ and Chance's team are both 5–1**, followed by Macon's **Coke(r) Caine** and Ryan Hutchins's **Lamborghini Murci** at 4–2. At the other end, Casey and Louis are both 1–5. The playoff race and the struggle to avoid last place are becoming two very different news beats.

The newsroom is reopening without promising an edition next Tuesday, Friday, or any other day that might interfere with actual life. **The Wire will publish the next story when there is a story worth filing.**
