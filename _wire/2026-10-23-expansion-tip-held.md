An anonymous submission has accused Larry Terrell and Frankie Yentz of resisting expansion of the AAA from ten to twelve teams. The author also connected the debate to Chance Robinson's commissioner campaign.

There is one substantial difficulty with this account: **no formal expansion proposal has been introduced**, and the newsroom has not verified that either Larry or Frankie opposed one.

Chance supports the idea. Casey Hutchins's position is currently unclear.

The email is evidence that someone wants this to become a political controversy. It is not, by itself, evidence that the alleged opposition exists.

The newsroom is seeking Larry's and Frankie's positions and the commissioner's view before reporting the dispute as an actual policy fight.

**AAA Insider finding:** The election has acquired a constitutional crisis without acquiring an official proposal.
