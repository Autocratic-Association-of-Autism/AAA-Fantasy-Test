Postseason honors

Every manager receives one AAA postseason rank. Championship playoff appearances count completed seasons with a top-six playoff seed (championship bracket), excluding consolation appearances and the in-progress 2026 season. Additional championships and podium finishes earn stars within their tier; there is no championship cap. Titles outrank runner-up finishes, which outrank third places.

Sacko Bracket

The Sacko is awarded to the loser of the consolation bracket, not automatically the last-place regular-season team. The league has confirmed the 2021–22 through 2025–26 recipients and punishments.
Punishment history for the 2021–22 through 2025–26 seasons is confirmed by the league. The Sacko goes to the consolation-bracket loser. The 2026–27 punishment has not yet been recorded.
